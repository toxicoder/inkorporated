# Board governance

**What's on this page**

- Board composition patterns for private and public-ready companies
- Committee structure (audit, compensation, nominating/governance)
- Cadence, materials, and CEO–Board relationship
- Link to BOARD role codes and Corporate Secretary

**What this enables**

- Investor-ready governance without improvisation
- Clear escalation from management to directors

## Composition (template)

- Independent directors majority as the company matures
- Skills matrix: finance, industry, security/privacy, GTM, technology
- Corporate Secretary owns minutes, calendars, and policy register

## Committees

| Committee | Typical remit |
| --- | --- |
| Audit | Financial reporting integrity, internal controls, auditor oversight |
| Compensation | Exec pay, equity plans, clawbacks |
| Nominating / Governance | Board composition, evaluations, governance policies |

## Cadence

- Regular meetings quarterly at minimum; special meetings for material events
- Pre-read materials ≥5 business days when possible
- Executive sessions without management periodically
