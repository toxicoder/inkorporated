# Go-to-market strategy

**What's on this page**

- Motion options (PLG, sales-led, hybrid)
- Segmentation and packaging philosophy
- Metrics: pipeline, win rate, CAC payback
- Alignment with product and support capacity

**What this enables**

- GTM that does not outrun delivery or support

## Default hybrid motion

- Self-serve for low ACV
- Sales-assisted for mid-market
- Enterprise AE + SE + CS for high ACV / regulated

Coordinate with [Financial strategy](financial_strategy.md) unit economics gates.
