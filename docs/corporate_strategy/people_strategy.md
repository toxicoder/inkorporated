# People strategy

**What's on this page**

- Talent density philosophy
- Leveling and compensation principles
- Remote/hybrid norms
- Link to policies and VP People roles

**What this enables**

- Coherent hiring and performance systems as headcount scales

## Pillars

1. **Talent density over headcount**
2. **Dual-track careers** — see [Leveling](../organization/leveling_framework.md)
3. **Equity as ownership** — refreshers and clarity on liquidity
4. **Manager quality** — no untrained people managers at scale
5. **Inclusive defaults** — documented in policies and training
