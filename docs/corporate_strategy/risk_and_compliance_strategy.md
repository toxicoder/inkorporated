# Risk and compliance strategy

**What's on this page**

- Enterprise risk management posture
- Control framework themes (NIST CSF, ISO 27001-inspired)
- Roadmap stages for SOC 2 / ISO readiness (non-certified)
- Owners: CISO, CLO, Internal Audit

**What this enables**

- Sequencing compliance investment with customer and regulator demand

## Control domains

Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover (NIST CSF 2.0 themes).

Map policies under `docs/policies/` to these functions. Engage external auditors when seeking attestation—this monorepo provides operating templates only.
