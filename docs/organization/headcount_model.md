# Headcount model

**What's on this page**

- Stage-based role unlocks from seed to global enterprise
- Guidance for when to add control functions vs product builders
- Linkage to the ≥200 role saturation catalog

**What this enables**

- Capital-efficient hiring without skipping critical controls too late
- A scriptable “enterprise bootstrap” narrative for this monorepo

## Stages

| Stage | Approx HC | Focus | Must-have functions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seed | 1–15 | Product-market fit | Founders, full-stack, design/PM hybrid, founding GTM |
| Series A | 15–50 | Repeatable delivery | Eng managers, first SRE/security, CS, finance ops |
| Series B | 50–150 | Scale systems | VP layer, platform, data, legal/people partners, FO formalization if needed |
| Series C | 150–500 | Multi-product / multi-geo | Directors, regional leads, GRC, RevOps, support tiers |
| Pre-IPO / public | 500–2000+ | Controls & global ops | Board committees staffed, internal audit, IR, deep compliance |
| Global enterprise | 2000+ | Portfolio & regions | Full REGN structure, deep specialization, 200+ catalog active |

## Sequencing rules

1. **Never scale GTM faster than support and reliability capacity.**
2. **Hire security and privacy before regulated enterprise deals.**
3. **Family Office** scales with founder/executive load — integrated under CEO when active.
4. **Cyborgs** can stub unfilled human seats for process design and simulation; CRITICAL actions still require human approval.
