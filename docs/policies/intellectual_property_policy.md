## Purpose
This policy clarifies the ownership and protection of intellectual property (IP) created by employees and contractors.

## Ownership
*   **Company IP**: All inventions, designs, software, documentation, and other works created by employees during the course of their employment belong to the Company.
*   **Prior IP**: Employees must disclose any IP they owned prior to joining the Company to avoid conflicts.

## Protection
*   Employees must take all necessary steps to protect the Company's IP, including maintaining confidentiality.
*   Employees may not use Company IP for personal gain or outside business activities without prior written approval.

## Open Source
Contributions to open source projects must follow the Open Source Policy.
