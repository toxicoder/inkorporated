## Purpose
This policy guides employees on the responsible use of social media to protect the Company's reputation.

## Guidelines
*   **Personal Use**: Employees are free to use social media for personal purposes, but should clarify that their views are their own and not the Company's.
*   **Confidential Information**: Do not share confidential or proprietary information on social media.
*   **Professionalism**: Be respectful and professional when discussing work-related topics.
*   **Endorsements**: If you endorse the Company's products, you must disclose your employment status.

## Prohibited Content
*   Hate speech, harassment, or threats.
*   Content that violates the Code of Conduct.
